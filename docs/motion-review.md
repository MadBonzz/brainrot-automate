# Animation review

Blender 4.3.2 on Linux played 54 new performances on a saved character from the original rigid cast. Each clip was rendered at 12 fps from a three-quarter and a side view. Measurements at 12 and 24 fps checked the authored poses, delayed starts, one-shot holds or repeated cycles, and a late-cue shot. Three two-actor shots also passed through the normal animation renderer on fresh characters.

The first gesture pass exposed missing elbow and spine pivots. The first footwork pass put shoes as far as 0.13 scene units below the floor and let a stance foot slide 0.59 units. A later check found a designated supporting shoe could rise 0.19 units while the other shoe stayed down. The final flat-floor checks measured 0.010 units of sole clearance and under 0.001 units of horizontal drift during the specified stance intervals. Targeted hand checks used a tolerance of 0.075 scene units, with a largest measured miss of 0.020.

The timestamps below are the chapter starts of the 2 minute 11.5 second review reel. Every row passed at both frame rates. The additional measurements identify contact tests applied to that performance. Pantomime clips use an imaginary object. Actual prop attachment and contact belong to the staged shot.
